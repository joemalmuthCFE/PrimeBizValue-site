Confidentiality

Your financials stay yours

You're handing over real numbers about your business to get a real answer. Here's exactly what we do — and don't do — with that information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your financial information. Not to data brokers, not to advertisers, not to anyone. Full stop.
  • We don't share it with outside parties beyond what's needed to run the Service (see "Where your data actually goes" below).

What we retain

  • The financial information you submit — retained for our internal use.
  • Contact info — your name, email, and phone number if you provide it, so we can send your report and respond if you reach out.
  • Purchase history — that you bought a report and when, so we can handle receipts, support questions, and (if you've opted in) let you know about relevant updates or franchise partner offers.

Where your data actually goes

To read and structure the financials you submit, we use Anthropic's Claude AI to parse the numbers into the recast. That's the one place your financial data is processed by an outside party — it's used solely to generate your report, and isn't used to train Anthropic's models.

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Your own device

The tool saves your in-progress recast locally in your own browser, so you don't lose your work if you navigate away mid-session. That data lives on your device, not on our servers, and clearing it is entirely in your control.
