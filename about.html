About PrimeBizValue

We are asking you to upload your profit and loss statement to a website. That is a real thing to ask a stranger for. This page is everything you should want to know before you do it: who we are, where our numbers come from, what we will not do, and how to reach a person.

What we make

One thing: a valuation document for a small business, produced from your own financials, in minutes. It contains an itemized recast of your earnings, the derivation of the multiple applied, a valuation range, and a breakdown of what you would actually net at closing — plus, in the Detailed report, a debt-service coverage screen for buyers who intend to borrow.

We are deliberately narrow. We work on Main Street businesses — roughly, businesses worth under $2M. We do not do mid-market M&A, we do not do litigation work, and we do not broker deals.

If you want to see exactly what you get before you spend anything, we published a complete worked example: read the sample report.

Why this exists

There are more than twenty free business-valuation calculators online. Every one of them is paid for by someone: a broker who wants your listing, a bank that wants your loan, a marketplace that wants its commission. They are not dishonest, but they are not neutral either, and none of them produces a document you can hand to anyone.

The thing that is genuinely scarce is not a number. It is a number with its work shown — something a franchisor's transfer desk, a lender's credit analyst, a partner, or a buyer's accountant can read, check, and argue with. That is what we sell, and it is the only thing we sell. Nobody here earns a commission on your transaction, because we are not part of your transaction.

On top of the size band we apply adjustment factors for earnings trend, owner dependency, customer concentration, recurring revenue, lease position, and documentation quality. Those weights are ours, not an industry standard, and the report prints every one of them so you can see what moved your multiple and disagree with it if you think we got it wrong.

One thing we will not do is publish a single “industry multiple.” Anyone who tells you every business in your industry sells for the same multiple is guessing, and the guess is usually flattering.

What we refuse to do

This is the honest heart of the page, and the part we would most like you to hold us to.

  • We will never call you. There is no sales team, no “valuation advisor,” no one whose job is to follow up. You will get your report and the emails required to deliver it, and that is all. Every free valuation tool in this market asks for your phone number and then routes you to a person. We do not want your phone number.
  • We will never sell, rent, or pass on your details or your financials — not to brokers, not to lenders, not to franchise consultants, not to anyone. Your P&L is not our product.
  • We will never claim an accreditation we do not hold. Our “Prime Valuation Certification” is our own internal standard. It is not issued or recognised by any professional body. We say that on the certification page in plain language rather than hiding it in a footnote.
  • We will never imply our report is SBA-compliant. It is not, and it structurally cannot be. Under SBA SOP 50 10 8, when the financed intangible portion of an acquisition exceeds $250,000, the independent valuation must be ordered by the lender from an appraiser holding an ASA, CBA, ABV, CVA, AVA, or BCA credential, independent of loan production. You cannot buy that from us or from any other online tool. What we sell is a pre-application screen — an early read on whether a deal is likely to clear coverage, before you spend weeks finding out.
  • We will not sell into divorce, probate, estate tax, or litigation. Courts and the IRS require credentialed work. An automated report is not admissible and buying one would waste your money. Email us and we will point you toward the right kind of professional instead.
  • We will not inflate a number to win your business. We have nothing to win. We are not seeking your listing, we take no commission, and we receive no fee, referral payment, or other consideration from any broker, lender, marketplace, or franchisor.
  • We will not manufacture social proof. Which brings us to the next section.

Why there are no testimonials on this site

You will notice we display no customer quotes, no star ratings, no “trusted by 3,000 business owners,” and no logos. That is deliberate. We are a young business and we are not going to invent the one thing that would be easiest to invent. When we have customers who are willing to be quoted by name, with permission, we will publish exactly what they said and nothing more.

In the meantime, the only honest substitutes for social proof are the ones on this page: show the method, cite the data, publish the deliverable, and state plainly what the product cannot do. A complete example of the report is here. Judge us on that.

How your financials are handled

The specifics, without marketing language:

  • Your uploaded P&L is parsed by an automated system so the line items can be pre-filled for you. Our AI processing is performed via Anthropic.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Report delivery and account email is sent through Resend.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use your financials to build a marketing list.
  • Retention, deletion, and the full list of subprocessors are set out on the confidentiality page and in our privacy policy.
  • Deletion on request. Email hello@primebizvalue.com from the address you used and ask us to delete your uploaded documents and data. We will confirm when it is done.
