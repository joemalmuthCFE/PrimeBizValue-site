About

Why we built this

Most business owners walk into a resale conversation with a guess instead of a number. We wanted to fix that.

PrimeBizValue started from a simple, recurring frustration: franchise owners and buyers making six- and seven-figure decisions with nothing more than a hallway conversation or a broker's back-of-napkin estimate to go on.

The team behind PrimeBizValue has years of franchise development experience, watching the same pattern play out again and again: owners underselling because they didn't know their real number, buyers overpaying because no one showed them the math, and both sides missing the kind of clear-eyed valuation conversation that should be table stakes before any deal gets done.

What makes this different

  • Real methodology, not a black box. We use the same SDE-recast approach brokers use, benchmarked against actual Main Street transaction data (BizBuySell, IBBA/M&A Source), not a made-up formula.
  • Built for franchise resale specifically. The recast worksheet reflects how franchise financials actually work, not a generic small-business template.
  • No subscription, no upsell. One flat fee, one report, delivered in minutes.

What this isn't

We're upfront about this: PrimeBizValue is a directional, informational estimate — not a certified appraisal, and not a substitute for a CPA, licensed business appraiser, or attorney when real money and paperwork are on the line. It's built to get you a real, defensible starting number fast, not to replace professional advice when you need it.
