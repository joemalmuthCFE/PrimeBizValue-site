Questions people actually ask

When do I need a certified business appraisal instead of an automated report?

Whenever a third party with authority has to rely on the number: a court, the IRS, an SBA lender above the intangible threshold, an ESOP trustee, or the other side of a dispute. Divorce, probate, estate and gift tax, shareholder litigation, ESOP work, and lender-ordered SBA valuations all require a credentialed appraiser. If your situation is on that list, do not buy our report - it will not be accepted, and we would rather tell you now.

What does a certified business appraisal cost?

A full credentialed narrative appraisal typically runs $3,000 to $15,000. Divorce and litigation work, where the appraiser may have to defend the report in a deposition or at trial, commonly runs $7,500 to $15,000 and up. An independent valuation obtained for SBA acquisition financing is generally lighter and commonly runs $1,500 to $5,000 with a one-to-three-week turnaround.

What credentials should a business appraiser have?

The credentials the SBA recognises are a useful shortlist: ASA, CBA, ABV, CVA, AVA, and BCA. The American Society of Appraisers (ASA), NACVA (CVA and AVA), and the AICPA (ABV) each publish member directories you can search directly. For court work, ask specifically about testimony experience in your jurisdiction, not just about letters after the name.

Can I use a cheap report first and a certified appraisal later?

Often, yes, and it is frequently the rational order - as a screen, never as a substitute. If a fast documented read tells you the deal does not work or the price is nowhere near your expectation, you have saved the appraisal fee entirely. If it tells you the deal is live, you commission the credentialed work already knowing roughly where you stand. What you must not do is submit the cheap one where the credentialed one is required.
