“Why would I pay for this?”

Fair. Here is the honest answer to each version of that question.

“I can get a valuation free from a broker.”

You can, and you should — get several. But understand what you're being given. A broker's opinion of value is not a product they sell; it's bundled into the commission they earn if you list with them. That's a real service, honestly offered, and it is also produced by someone whose income depends on winning your listing and on the deal closing.

Two consequences worth knowing. A broker will not hand you a documented add-back worksheet you can take somewhere else. And if you are the buyer, there is no free valuation in this market written for you — every one of them is sell-side.

We take no commission and we don't want your listing. We have no stake in your number being high. That is the entire reason to pay for one. The long version, written fairly to brokers →

“Why not just ask ChatGPT?”

Honestly? For a rough number, do. Paste your P&L into a chatbot and it will apply a multiple to a rough SDE, and it will be roughly right, and it is free. We would rather say that than pretend otherwise.

Here is what you won't have afterwards. An itemized recast tied to source documents, with a note on which add-backs will not survive diligence. A multiple tied to a published transaction dataset and a specific size band, with the derivation shown. A debt-service screen. A net-cash-to-seller waterfall. And a document — with a date, a method, and a stated basis — that you can put in front of a franchisor's transfer desk or a credit analyst.

A chatbot gives you an answer. This gives you something to hand to the person who has to say yes. The four places AI stops being the right tool →

“Shouldn't I just get a real appraisal?”

Sometimes, yes — and we will tell you when.

If you need a valuation for a court, a divorce, an estate tax filing, or an IRS matter, do not buy this. It will not be accepted. Engage a credentialed appraiser (ASA, CVA, or ABV). Email us and we'll point you in the right direction.

If you are borrowing through the SBA and the financed intangible portion exceeds $250,000, your lender is required to order an independent valuation from a credentialed appraiser — you cannot buy that one yourself, from us or from anyone. Formal appraisals of that kind typically run into the thousands of dollars and take one to three weeks.

What we sell is the step before all of that: a fast, documented, defensible read you can act on this afternoon, for $199. If it tells you the deal doesn't work, you've saved the appraisal fee entirely. When you need the credentialed appraisal instead of us →
