This Privacy Policy is incorporated by reference into, and forms part of, our Terms of Service. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Privacy Policy — including any claim relating to the collection, use, disclosure, or handling of information — is subject to the dispute-resolution (including binding arbitration), governing-law, and limitation-of-liability provisions of the Terms of Service, which apply to this Privacy Policy as if fully set forth here.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the PrimeBizValue website, valuation process, customer communications, marketing forms, and related online services.

PrimeBizValue uses automated technologies, which may include artificial intelligence and machine-learning systems, to assist in processing information submitted through the Service.

  • extracting financial line items;

At launch, PrimeBizValue uses commercial artificial-intelligence services provided by Anthropic, including its Claude API, for certain processing functions. Information submitted for AI-assisted analysis may be transmitted to and processed by Anthropic to provide those services to PrimeBizValue.

Third-party AI providers may change their technologies, policies, and contractual terms over time, and PrimeBizValue may change AI or technology providers as the Service evolves. We will update this Privacy Policy when a material change to our processing practices requires disclosure.

Automated analysis can make mistakes. Limitations applicable to PrimeBizValue valuation reports are described in our Terms of Service.
